Why Mercosur

Direct from Mercosur Dairy Belt

Uruguay and Argentina have a long tradition of dairy export to global markets. The Mercosur dairy belt produces to international food safety standards with full traceability from farm to export container.

SA currently imports FCMP predominantly through trading intermediaries. JMR works directly with the cooperatives, creating a shorter supply chain and more transparent pricing.

Medium heat FCMP for coffee creamers, recombined dairy, and food manufacturing is our primary grade. Halal, FSSC 22000, and ISO 9001 certified producers available.
