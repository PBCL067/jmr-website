Why Mercosur

The Classic Displacement Opportunity

China supplies the majority of SA's soya lecithin needs. Argentina's Rosario crushing corridor processes over 40 million tonnes of soybeans annually, producing food grade lecithin at scale with full export infrastructure.

The import tariff on lecithin is FREE under HS 2923.90, meaning there is no cost barrier to switching origin. The opportunity is structural and underexplored.

JMR is actively developing the Argentina-to-SA lecithin route, working with certified producers in the Rosario soy complex. This is a live sourcing project, not a future ambition.
