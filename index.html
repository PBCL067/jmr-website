What We Source

Our Product Range

From modified starches to dairy ingredients and edible oils, sourced directly from certified Mercosur producers.

Industrial Legume & Pulse Derivatives

Fine-mesh chickpea flour, lentil flours (red/green), and export-grade whole pulses. Directly sourced from Argentine producers for industrial baking, gluten-free formulations, and protein enrichment.

Modified Corn Starch E1422

Acetylated distarch adipate, ideal for sauces, dairy, processed foods and bakery applications requiring heat and freeze-thaw stability. Origin: Argentina / Brazil.

Full Cream Milk Powder

Medium heat FCMP for coffee creamers, recombined dairy and food manufacturing. Sourced from certified Uruguayan and Argentine cooperatives. Origin: Uruguay / Argentina.

Soya Lecithin

Food grade soya lecithin from Argentina's Rosario crushing corridor, the world's largest soy processing complex. Competitive FOB pricing, Halal available. Origin: Argentina.

Sunflower & Soybean Oil

Refined edible oils from Argentina's major oilseed processors. Argentina is the world's 2nd largest sunflower oil exporter. Origin: Argentina / Brazil.
