OUR STORY

Connecting South American Agriculture to Global Food Manufacturers

Built on 10 years of agribusiness experience across Latin America and Africa.

Who We Are

Buenos Aires to the Gulf & GCC Corridors

JMR Global Group was founded by South Africans based in Buenos Aires with over a decade of experience building agribusiness trade flows between Latin America and the world.

We specialize in sourcing export-grade agro-commodities and industrial food ingredients—including fine-mesh chickpea flour, red and green lentil flours, modified starches, soya lecithin, and dairy powders. Originating directly from certified Argentine and South American processing facilities, we manage complete supply chain routing into major GCC trade ports like CFR Salalah and Jebel Ali.

Our model is simple: identify where South Africa is overpaying or undersupplied, find the best Mercosur producer to fill that gap, and manage the relationship from first contact to delivered product.

How We Work

Our Approach

🌎

Presence On Both Continents

Our team in Mercosur maintains close relationships with established producers, while our team in South Africa understands the needs of local food manufacturers. We operate with genuine market knowledge on both sides of the Atlantic.

🤝

Building New Trade Routes

We connect South American producers directly with Gulf food manufacturers and industrial distributors, offering local buyers a credible alternative to traditional EU and Chinese suppliers, often with stronger pricing and shorter lead times.

🚢

End to End Facilitation

From initial introduction through to delivered product, we manage the full process: specifications, certifications, logistics and communication across the Atlantic, allowing both parties to focus on their core business.

Our Markets

South America to GCC & Middle East

We source exclusively from Mercosur, Argentina, Brazil, Uruguay, Paraguay and Chile. These are among the world's most competitive producers of food ingredients, yet many are completely absent from the South African market.

South Africa is our primary end market, but we work across the continent, Nigeria, Kenya, Egypt, Ghana, Morocco and East Africa.

The opportunity is structural: Mercosur produces at world-competitive prices, South Africa imports from more expensive origins. We close that gap.
