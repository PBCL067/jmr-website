WHO WE ARE

Built on Nearly Two Decades of Cross-Border Trade

JMR Global was established by Matt Callcott-Stevens, whose cross-border trading career began in 2009 managing high-volume beverage and agricultural trade flows between emerging markets in Asia and Africa.

Working directly with agricultural producers to establish direct trade channels into neighbouring markets and the Gulf region provided a firsthand understanding of GCC food security requirements, industrial ingredient specifications, and import standards.

After relocating to Argentina in the mid-2010s, Matt recognized the unmatched agricultural capacity of the Mercosur bloc—Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay, and Uruguay. JMR (Jewels of the Mercosur Region) was created to connect these farming regions directly with food manufacturers in the Middle East.

OUR STRATEGIC FOCUS

Why We Specialize in Pulses & Derivatives

As traditional cash crops face rising input costs and unpredictable climate conditions, pulse derivatives offer a sustainable, high-protein food source for global buyers.

Climate Resilience

Unlike water-intensive traditional cash crops, legumes thrive in temperamental weather conditions. They offer essential stability for long-term food security in high-demand import regions like the GCC.

Soil Health & Sustainability

Pulses naturally fix atmospheric nitrogen into soil, reducing synthetic fertilizer dependence and protecting long-term agricultural yield across South American farming communities.

Economic Efficiency

Soaring production costs for traditional grains pass heavy inflation downstream to consumers. Pulse derivatives provide high-protein ingredient solutions at predictable cost structures.

Partner with JMR Global

Direct producer sourcing, origin oversight, and trade facilitation between South America and the Gulf.
