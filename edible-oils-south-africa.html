Why Mercosur

A Tariff Advantage That is Not Fully Exploited

Under the MERCOSUR-SACU preferential trade agreement, Argentine sunflower oil attracts a 4% import duty versus 10% MFN for all other origins. This is a structural cost advantage that the market has not fully priced in.

Argentina is the world's second largest sunflower oil exporter with massive crushing capacity in the Rosario and Bahia Blanca corridors. Export infrastructure, quality and certifications are well established.

JMR provides direct access to Argentina's major oilseed processors for bulk sunflower and soybean oil, in flexi-bag or ISO tank, delivered to Durban.
